Ready-to-use copy for bios, outreach, pitches, or anywhere you need to
describe ThemeCrown in someone else's inbox or feed.

## One-liner (tagline)

> A fairer way to get your Framer template discovered.

## Elevator pitch (2 sentences)

> ThemeCrown is a pay-to-rank leaderboard for Framer template authors. Bid
> $1 or more to boost your template's visibility — but the ranking formula
> flattens out the more you spend, so a $1 bid still gets a real shot
> against the biggest budgets, and every board resets weekly for a fresh
> shot at #1.

## Short "About" paragraph

> ThemeCrown is a leaderboard where Framer template authors bid to get
> discovered — starting at just $1. Unlike a straight pay-to-win auction,
> the ranking formula gives early dollars outsized weight and flattens out
> for big spenders, so a small bidder always has a real shot, not just
> whoever has the deepest pockets. Boards reset every Monday, so there's
> no such thing as a permanent #1 — just a fresh, fair shot every week.
